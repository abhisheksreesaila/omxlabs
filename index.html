Every number and every detail

One tap, start to finish.

One language, Mojo, from the button to the GPU kernel. On Linux and the Mac, nothing is serialized and nothing waits on a server.

1 · you

Tap “Sum” in the chart's title and pick “Median”.

2 · the app

The tap is a message. update() changes the chart's state; view() describes the new screen.

3 · the engine

The group-by and median run as GPU kernels over all 1M rows, in the same process.

4 · the screen

Only what changed is redrawn, and the bars spring to their new heights.
