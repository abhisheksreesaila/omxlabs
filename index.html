Why it's different

Everything in one place. Three ways that shows.

Most data work is spread across separate programs, with copies and exports between them. omxplorer keeps it together.

01 · GPU native

One program, from your data to the screen.

What it means
Your rows are loaded onto the graphics card once and stay there. The calculations run where the data is, and the same program draws the screen.
The difference
Nothing is copied, so a chart over a million rows redraws in 0.6 ms.
How it works
02 · Agent native

One set of controls, for you and your agent.

What it means
Every button is also a tool an AI agent can call by name, such as Claude Code, over MCP, the standard agents already speak.
The difference
The agent calls the button directly, and can do nothing you couldn't: a disabled button isn't on its list.
How it works
03 · Multimodal

One sheet for every kind of data.

What it means
Tables, charts and video sit side by side and can be joined: a video's frames become rows, so footage is searched like a sheet.
The difference
Stop a video on a frame, read every sensor at that instant, then ask in words for every run like it.
